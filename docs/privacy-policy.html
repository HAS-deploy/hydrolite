HydroLite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

HydroLite is a simple hydration-logging app. Your hydration data stays on your device; we collect only anonymous product analytics so we can improve the app.

What we collect

HydroLite sends anonymous product analytics to PostHog (US cloud, project 389134). Per event, that includes:

  • The event name (e.g. paywall.viewed, paywall.purchase_success, screen.viewed, settings.toggled) and non-identifying event properties such as the screen name, user segment (anonymous/free/trial/premium/lifetime), days since install, and locale.
  • A random per-install identifier generated by the PostHog SDK. It is not your Apple ID, email, device name, phone number, IDFA, or any ad identifier.
  • Your app version and build number, your device model and iOS version, and an is_review_env flag that marks events from TestFlight or App Review sandbox builds so we can exclude them.
  • For in-app purchases: the StoreKit product ID, the transaction ID, and the price PostHog receives from the device. We never see your payment method, Apple ID, or billing address — Apple handles the transaction.

We never collect the amounts, timestamps, drink types, notes, or any content of your hydration logs; we do not collect contacts, photos, health data, precise or coarse location, or an advertising identifier. The PostHog iOS SDK is configured with autocapture off, screen-view autocapture off, session replay off, and personProfiles = .never; only the events listed above are emitted.

Identification after purchase

After your first in-app purchase on this device, we associate that install's analytics events with a pseudonymous identifier derived from a hash of Apple's identifier-for-vendor (IDFV): we take MD5(IDFV), keep only the first 12 hex characters, and prefix it with anon_. The raw IDFV itself is never sent. The IDFV is the same value for all apps from this developer on this device, so this pseudonymous identifier lets us count how many of our apps the same device has paid for; it is also stable across reinstalls as long as another of our apps remains installed. Apple's App Privacy taxonomy classifies IDFV-derived values as a Device ID, so we disclose it as such on the App Store listing. The pseudonymous identifier is never combined with your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or payment details, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with data brokers. Turning off anonymous analytics (Settings → Data → Anonymous usage analytics) stops this identification from being sent.

What lives on your device

Your settings, saved presets, and drink logs are stored locally using standard iOS preferences. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Nothing about your logs is sent anywhere.

Opt-out of analytics

You can turn analytics off at any time: open HydroLite → Settings → Data → toggle Anonymous usage analytics off. No further events will be sent from your device after you opt out.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, HydroLite schedules local iOS notifications. Nothing about your reminders is sent to us or anyone else.

Subscriptions & purchases

HydroLite offers two auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and yearly), each with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, and a one-time lifetime unlock. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period and may be managed in your Apple ID account settings. Apple processes every purchase; we never see your payment method, Apple ID, or billing address. We operate no purchase server and do no server-side receipt validation; the only purchase details we receive are the product ID, transaction ID and price in the anonymous analytics described above.

Tracking & advertising

HydroLite does not run ads, does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, does not share any data with data brokers, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children

HydroLite is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, call it out in the app's release notes.
